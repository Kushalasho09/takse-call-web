PART 1: TEST CSV IMPORT ON WEB (CONNECTS TO BACKEND FIRESTORE)
================================================================================

1. START THE WEB APP:
   - Open terminal:
     cd "/Volumes/SSD/kushal_flutter/Takse Call/takse_call_web"
   - Run in Chrome:
     flutter run -d chrome

2. GO TO IMPORT LEADS:
   - In left menu, click "Leads".
   - In top sub-bar, click "Import Leads".

3. SELECT OR LOAD CSV (Step 1):
   - Option A: Click [Load Sample CSV (kushal - 9978217711)] (Instant 1-Click)
   - Option B: Click [Select File] and choose:
     /Volumes/SSD/kushal_flutter/Takse Call/takse_call_leads_sample.csv
   - Click [Next: Map Fields ->].

4. MAP & VALIDATE (Step 2 & Step 3):
   - On Step 2 (Map Fields): Click [Next: Validate CSV Data ->].
   - On Step 3 (Validate):
     Verify Row 1 displays:
     Name: kushal (Target Lead)
     Phone: 9978217711
     Company: Takse Enterprises
     Status: Pass
   - Click [Next: Actions & Duplicate Rules ->].

5. CONFIRM & IMPORT TO BACKEND (Step 4 & Step 5):
   - On Step 4 (Actions): Click [Review Summary & Import ->].
   - On Step 5 (Summary): Click the green button [Confirm & Complete Import].
   - RESULT: All leads are saved directly into Firebase Cloud Firestore!

PART 2: TEST ON MOBILE APP (ANDROID PHONE)
================================================================================

1. START THE MOBILE APP:
   - Connect your Android phone with USB Debugging enabled.
   - Open terminal:
     cd "/Volumes/SSD/kushal_flutter/Takse Call/takse_call_app"
   - Run on your device:
     flutter run

2. ENABLE OVERLAY PERMISSION (One-Time Setup):
   - In the app, tap "Call History" tab.
   - At the top, see the banner "Display Over Other Apps" -> Tap [Enable].
   - Turn toggle ON for "Allow display over other apps" (or "Appear on top").
   - Press Back button to return to app.

3. VERIFY IMPORTED LEAD IN APP:
   - Tap "My Leads" tab at the bottom.
   - You will see "kushal" (9978217711) at the top of your leads list with:
     * Name: kushal
     * Number: 9978217711
     * Company: Takse Enterprises
     * Status: Positive

4. MAKE A CALL & TEST POST-CALL DIALOG:
   - On Kushal's lead card, tap the green Call icon.
   - Your phone will dial 9978217711.
   - Cut / End the call.
   - You land back on your normal phone system dialer.
   - The native floating popup dialog immediately appears on top of your dialer!
   - Dialog shows:
     * Caller: kushal
     * Phone: 9978217711
     * 4 Status options: Positive, Call Back, Negative, Not Pickup Call
     * Add Note field
     * 3 Buttons: [Save & Exit], [Save & Connect], [Save & Add Lead]

5. UPDATE STATUS & SAVE:
   - Tap "Positive" (or "Call Back" and pick date/time).
   - Type note: "Interested in demo".
   - Tap [Save & Exit].
   - The dialog closes smoothly and saves to Firestore backend.

PART 4: TEST GOOGLE SHEETS & EXCEL CONNECTOR (API KEY & DIRECT SYNC)
================================================================================

1. OPEN CONNECTORS HUB ON WEB:
   - In left menu, click "Leads".
   - In top sub-bar, click "Import Leads".
   - Click the "Automated Connectors Hub" tab.
   - Click the first card: "Google Sheet (Import Leads)".

2. CONFIGURE GOOGLE SHEET:
   - Sheet URL: Paste your Google Sheet URL (or leave default test sheet).
   - Google Cloud API Key (Optional): Enter your GCP API Key if private/restricted, 
     OR leave blank if sheet sharing is set to "Anyone with the link can view".
   - Sheet Range: e.g. Sheet1!A1:Z500
   - Routing: Choose Round-Robin or Single Dedicated Telecaller.

3. TEST & IMPORT:
   - Click [Test Connection & Preview Sheet Rows]:
     -> Live status fetches and displays row count and headers preview.
   - Click [Import & Sync to Pipeline]:
     -> Leads are automatically converted, assigned to telecallers, and saved
        directly into Cloud Firestore and your live My Leads table!
